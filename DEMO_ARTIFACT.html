What the data says

  • Just over half the monitored estate is already off pathway. 5.75M of 11.01M m², across 613 of 1,200 assets. This is a 2025 position, not a 2030 projection.
  • Exposure is concentrated in one client. Rhenus Immobilien GmbH accounts for 36.8% of all stranded area, three times the next client, and 98% of its own portfolio is off pathway. That is not a data error: a German care portfolio running on gas boilers and electric resistance heating cannot clear a 62 kgCO2e/m² threshold when the German grid factor is 0.366 kgCO2e/kWh against 0.058 in France.
  • Within that client, the problem has a shape. Nursing homes are about 80% of its stranded area: gas-heated homes contribute 1.11M m², and 56 electric-resistance homes carry the worst intensity gap in the entire book at roughly 100 kgCO2e/m² above threshold.
  • The commercial exposure is legible. €1.48M of €2.47M in subscription value sits with the five most stranded clients. Rhenus alone is €368,000, and its contract renews on 14 September 2026.
  • No single system could answer this. Consumption came from metering and utility invoices, floor area and sector from the client asset register, thresholds from external CRREM pathway data, and subscription value from the commercial system.
